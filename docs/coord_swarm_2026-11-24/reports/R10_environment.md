# R10 — Environment / Season / Weather / Time-of-day

**Bottom line.** Donor skirmish *does* ship a full environment subsystem (env.cfg → time/colour-table/skydome/clouds/flares/lights/PFX), 3 seasons with real colour-table + mesh/material/terrain swaps, weather bands 0–99 with wind + snow PFX, and five ambient beds. But its **day/night manager is explicitly disabled** (`scripts/lib/init.script:99 SetDateTimeManagerEnabled(false)`, confirmed by a whole-data-tree scan: only `env/env.cfg:15`, `env/lights/light.ref:116 DateTimeEnable = False`, `init.script:99`; no re-enable). Port has season *selection*, tint/winter art, a serious donor-equation water shader, and 2 of 5 ambient beds; it has **no clouds, weather state, snow, rain, sky/fog palette, lens flare or day-night cycle**. Rain is **not** a donor skirmish feature (no rain PFX/sound found) — do not ticket it as parity.
