## 6. Visual judgement — honest, post-coordinator-review

**My earlier "PASS" was too generous and is withdrawn.** I re-read the final
compare PNG with the image tool after each correction. Honest assessment:

**Corrected (all four flagged proxy failure modes addressed):**
- **Hard tree outlines** — alpha blurred 2px at 1024² (≈1px at 512²); no hard
  cut-out stencil edge remains.
- **Black iconic trees** — whole-crown frost (0.80/0.58/0.74) turns dark green
  sprites into light grey snow-laden shapes with branch gaps still visible;
  the near-black silhouettes are gone. An intermediate 0.92 frost produced
  featureless pale "ghosts" and was reduced on visual evidence.
- **Missing contact shadow / AO** — each conifer now has a soft cool elliptical
  shadow toward lower-left (sun upper-right), grounding it on the snow.
- **Uniform procedural speckle** — preserve-mask Gaussian blur (radius 0.85 at
  1024²) lowers hf from 8.39 to **6.75** and L std from 21.68 to **13.99**,
  both moving toward the donor snow_open anchors (hf 5.42, std 11.08), while
  exact whites/near-whites stay byte-identical.

**Residual defect (not corrected within this piece's asset budget):**
- The conifers still read as **simplified sprite shapes**, not the layered,
  drooping, photographically snow-laden spruces of reference #1. The port
  contains no dedicated frosted-spruce sprite; the frost is procedural over a
  dark-green icon. The rock footprints also remain soft grey masses rather
  than individually shaded boulders. This is the known 2D-port structural gap
  (T6 plan A10), not something a tint or blur can close.

**Verdict: CONDITIONAL PASS / not donor-equal.** All numeric criteria and the
27-block wiring gate pass; the four specifically flagged schematic defects are
materially reduced and measured; the residual sprite schematicity is openly
reported. Per "metrics never override visual review", the final call is deferred
to owner visual review at combo/full-map stage (Q6).
