## 2. Visual A/B judgement (honest)

The compare sheet's donor panel is a **labelled composite of genuine donor
tile art + donor-mesh rock sprites** (donor `ground1.dds` centre crop
`(256,256,768,768)` plus four port-baked donor `.osm` rock sprites at matching
foot anchors). It contains **no minimap, UI text, units, buildings or
degenerate stretched strips**, satisfying the binding donor-crop rule via the
explicit composite route (T15 precedent). It is read-only donor art.

What I see when reading both final PNGs:

- The port reads as **real dirt/gravel terrain**, not diagram art: the ground
  carries clumped 2–7 px grey chips with bare gaps and tiny lower-left contact
  shadows; several donor-mesh stones of genuinely varied size (12–76 px) sit
  with soft directional shadows; one large outcrop enters from the top-left
  corner with a strong contact shadow and a faceted, non-silhouette edge.
- No hard black outlines, no iconic stencil sprites, no mirror-tiling /
  kaleidoscope artefacts, no uniform lattice, no missing contact shadows.
- The overall grade matches the donor panel closely: port mean RGB
  `(87.3,77.9,56.0)` vs donor panel `(88.4,79.3,58.5)` — within 1–2.5 levels.
  Port L-std 22.1 vs donor panel 27.6; port hi-freq 9.56 vs donor panel 13.66.
- **Honest residuals**: the port's grey chip field is denser and more uniform
  in tone than the clean donor dirt tile (the chips are a proxy for the
  donor's separately placed `d_stn_*` smallstone meshes, which the tile alone
  does not show); the donor panel's medium stones cluster more tightly and its
  largest boulder is slightly more central than the port's edge outcrop; the
  donor panel has a little more local contrast (L-std 27.6 vs 22.1). These are
  transfer items for combo/owner review, not silent gaps.

**Visual verdict: PASS (qualified by the three disclosed residuals).** The
piece is a credible donor-style stone scatter and is materially better than
the T2 baseline (two ~325 px centred boulders, a row of icon stones, green
cubes and an invalid unit-filled donor panel).
