## 5. Visual judgement — **FAIL** (governing verdict)

I read `t6_hill_ridge.png` and `t6_hill_ridge_compare.png` after the final render. The port
side is a **clean, smooth dark loaf with a thin bright rim, subtle pebbled texture and six
small conifers on near-uniform grass**. The donor crop shows a **broad, deeply and
irregularly striated escarpment with dense clustered trees, dark recesses and organic
grass contact**. The piece has a lit-top/shadowed-foot grade (verified numerically), an AO
collar, a cast shadow and no uniform procedural pattern — but the flank texture is far too
subtle and the rim too clean to read as an eroded 3D landscape. It also lacks donor-scale
tree density and tonal patches on the cap. Under "metrics never override visual review"
the **ticket verdict is FAIL**.

Iterations attempted and read back: (a) S-ribbon with straight uniform striations and hard
outline; (b) vertically striped panel; (c) rounded loaf with warped banding and rounded
ends; (d) final clustered rim trees + stronger irregular banding. (a) and (b) were rejected
for the exact "hard outline / uniform pattern" failure the coordinator warned about. The
renderer is left at (d), the best of the four, with the visual FAIL recorded honestly.

## 6. Open questions

1. **M1/M4/M5 classifiers.** The spec "grey-brown" test (`sat<0.4, B>0.55R`) also selects
   dark olive grass and cast-shadow pixels, giving a full-canvas bbox and inflated rock
   fraction (21%). A value floor + stricter sat cut raises the rock centroid to x≈0.37 but
   still yields ~10–14% whole-piece rock. Needs an owner-adopted canonical classifier or a
   hand mask; do not tune the piece to a broken metric.
2. **Legacy rock thresholds vs donor scale.** M4's whole-piece 2–7% was calibrated to the
   old ~2%-area thin face; a donor-scale broad flank necessarily occupies more of a 512²
   piece. Reconcile the acceptance band with the donor 8–18% rock-area visual estimate.
3. **M2 crest area 25% vs 30–45% band.** The top-quartile threshold selects the grass cap
   only; a different crest definition (lit rock cap + grass) may reach the band. Needs a
   definition decision, not a renderer change.
4. **Striation visibility.** Advisory M7 is 1.69× (<2×); stronger banding risks reverting
   to the rejected corduroy look. A heightfield/3D-relief renderer (not a 2D proxy) is
   likely needed for a true donor-grade eroded face.
5. **Heightfield scale (S01 context).** The donor grid is 256/320/480/640² with a 2×
   collision grid; the port is 160². This piece is a 2D visual proxy with **no height
   channel, collision or `blocked[]` output** — the "mask occupancy respected" claim is
   about the rendered footprint, not the engine relief. A dump-verifier ticket is required
   before any integration claim.
6. **Combo risk.** The six rim spruces are drawn in both render variants so M8's exact
   difference isolates the mandated tree; at combo scale their spacing may read as a row
   rather than a natural cluster.
