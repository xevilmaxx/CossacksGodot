## Honest visual judgement (metrics never override this)

I read the shipped `t6_field_crops.png`, the 3× `t6_field_crops_stalkzoom.png`,
the winter/depleted variants and the full `t6_field_crops_compare.png`.

- **Matches the donor read:** a hard-edged golden rectangle sitting on green
  grass; dense vertical stalk texture; ragged top/bottom tips; a subtle dark
  contact ring at the edge; solid interior (no grass holes); no black outlines,
  no sunflower/round heads, no mirror-tiling or repeated-stamp lattice. Both
  compare panels are clean and genuinely show the feature.
- **Gaps I will not hide:** the donor ears are visibly chunkier and carry
  brighter per-kernel glints; my field is finer-grained and flatter
  (interior hi-freq ≈10.3 vs donor #4 ≈11.5 at matched scale) and slightly more
  uniform in tone. The edge is a straight rectangular footprint with ragged
  tips, whereas the donor's in-game field is a rotated rectangle/parallelogram
  with a dirt clearing hole for the mill (not reproducible in a single 512²
  decal tile). The base is green grass, so it resembles donor #2 more than
  donor #4's dirt surround. These are fidelity gaps, not criterion failures.
- **Conclusion:** the proxy reads as real terrain, not diagram art. It earns a
  piece-level **PASS**; it is not an in-engine fix and must not be used to
  close the T2 `world.gd` wiring ticket.
