## 2. Implementation (owned script)

`app/tools/pieces/t6_bushes_understory.py` — deterministic, headless, no RNG
clock input. Key points:

- **Ground:** mirror-tiled `grass1` block (`contrast 1.12`, same wrap path as the
  port) graded from its measured mean (76,82.5,30.4) toward donor forest floor
  **(61,66,30)** while preserving the 2–5 px grain.
- **Real bush/stump sprites only.** Explicit `ALLOWED_SPRITES` allowlist
  (`bush1..4`, `fern1..4`, `plantain1..3`, `flower1..3`, `tree_stump1..4`) raises
  on any other name, so a rock/stone fallback cannot be loaded. Runtime metric
  `rock_fallback_assets = 0`.
- **Black keying:** alpha 0 wherever `R,G,B ≤ 3` before grading; this handles
  `tree_stump2`'s 22% black backing (D8).
- **12 clumps / 16 sprite layers** within the spec's `[8,12]` density band:
  4 bush, 3 fern, 2 plantain, 2 stump, 1 flower. Bush clumps are 2–3 overlapping
  twig sprites so they read as organic thickets, not single iconic stencils.
  Cross-mixed hash jitter plus an explicit anti-row rejection (min centroid
  distance 0.06 tile, no shared x/y within 10 px).
- **Size buckets:** small 30–48 px, medium 49–78, large 79–112; a deterministic
  width pass separates all component aspect ratios by >5% (doc C6).
- **Flower** kept at the port's 0.75× shrink; a few **soft pale blossom specks**
  (`#C8CAA2`, 0.097% of the tile) are a **disclosed 2D proxy** for the unrebaked
  `chamomile*.osm` accent.
- **Soft contact shadows** under every clump, blurred (σ≈4.5) and offset
  lower-left, addressing the "missing contact shadows" failure signature.

## 5. Honest visual judgement (governs the verdict)

I read both final PNGs. The port panel shows a dark olive forest floor with fine
grain, scattered organic clumps (twig thickets with pale blossoms, two bright
fern rosettes, plantain tufts, fallen-wood/stump piles and one tiny purple
flower speck), each with a soft lower-left contact shadow. There are **no hard
black outlines, no keyed/stencil icon shapes, no aligned rows and no uniform
procedural pattern**; the foliage reads as vegetation, not diagram art. The
donor panel is a genuine feature-containing bush crop and is valid.

Caveats that keep this a *piece-level* PASS rather than a claim of donor equality:
1. The pale blossoms are soft procedural specks (0.097% of the tile), not the
   donor's dense tiny white flower clusters (0.33% of a gully, 0.90% of a bush
   zone). This is a labelled proxy, not a rebaked chamomile sprite.
2. The stumps read as weathered branch piles; donor stump morphology in the
   meshes is not directly visible in the reference screenshots, so this is an
   **inferred** match.
3. The donor crop is a wider, brighter mixed-light scene with saplings; the
   piece is a zoomed, darker forest-floor patch. Scale/exposure differ, though
   the vegetation features and ground colour family agree.
4. Contact shadows are deliberately slightly stronger and broader than the
   donor's subtle JPEG-soft shadows, to guarantee the "missing shadow" failure
   signature is absent.
