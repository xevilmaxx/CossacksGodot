## 3. SPOT-CHECK FIXES (coordinator visual reviews — all resolved or adjudicated)

### FIX-1: hard black jagged band at the sand/water interface — FIXED

- **Defect (`observed` on the first `t6_water_shore.png`):** a hard black
  (`RGB≈0`) jagged band sat exactly on the sand→wash boundary. The donor
  has no black band.
- **Root cause:** the first shore revision painted land, wash, water and foam
  but **never assigned the sand band** (`rgb` starts as zeros; the `sand`
  boolean mask had no corresponding paint statement). The unpainted strip
  rendered as pure black. (Coordinator's "zero-alpha gap / band-geometry gap"
  diagnosis was essentially right; the gap was an unpainted band, not an
  alpha gap.)
- **Change:** the sand band is painted as a continuous dry→wet-sand gradient
  plus soft band weights (`_ss` smoothstep blends ±1.5px at wash boundaries,
  weights sum to 1 everywhere — no zero-coverage gap possible by construction;
  shoreline geometry smoothed: step 4, jitter ±6, 2 moving-average passes).
  Final sequence: dry sand → wet sand → wash → shallow water, organic edge.
- **Before/after evidence:** before `t6_water_shore.png` (10:54 revision, black
  band + sawtooth); after `app/shots/pieces/t6_water_shore.png` sha256
  `5418664b…22c184cc`. Measured: `black_px` (all channels <20) = **0**
  (`observed`); perpendicular profile at x=256:
  `(164,152,119)×6 → (166,154,121) → (161,149,116) → (178,183,169) →
  (197,211,204) → …` — continuous, no black, no step (`observed`); longest
  constant-x boundary run **1px** ≤ 8 (`derived`; was 7 with teeth, now 1).

### FIX-2: concentric ring pattern in the water body — REMOVED

- **Defect (`observed`):** the first shore water body showed procedural
  concentric rings (radial-sine term, centre lower-right) — radial synthesis,
  NOT donor waves.

### RE-CHECK (A): diagonal cross-hatch / moire — REMOVED (provenance documented)

- **Defect (`observed` by parent):** dense ~45° diagonal stripes in both water
  bodies; donor panels are smooth with faint broad banding.
- **Root cause (proven):** the donor crossing-swell term
  (`sin(p2.x*0.622+p2.y*0.622+…)`, frag:95) at piece scale gives a 5.6px
  diagonal fundamental. Body-only A/B (above) proves the swell is the sole
  stripe source: pure-fBm body = smooth, +swell = striped.
- **Change:** the visible body uses `swell_amp=0.0` — the directional swell is
  REMOVED from the body (its tilt spectrum stays in the NORMALS, which keep
  the donor-verbatim field for sparse glints). Verified: 4x centre zoom is
  smooth organic cellular + sparse glints; gradient-energy directionality
  ratio diag/axis = 0.966 (full-res) and 0.955 (256px thumbnail) — isotropic
  (`observed`; 1.0 = perfectly isotropic). Residual thumbnail grain is
  isotropic cellular mottle at reference variance, not directional striping.
- **Consequence (honest, adjudication requested):** the 5.6px fundamental's

  same pattern as the parent's S-M6/S-M7 N/A ruling.

## 4. Implementation summary (equations, constants, labels)

Donor equations implemented verbatim (`observed`, W01-pinned): `noise(p)`,
3-term f base with `m`, crossing sine swell, 9-octave loop (`p=m*p*0.947`,
`amp*=0.59`, `s*=-1.329`, shifts `(0.32t,0.24t)`/`(0.38t,-0.26t)`), central
differences at `dif=(0,0.01)` with `z=0.15`, per-pixel ray `rd=normalize(light-r0)`,
`grad=dot(n,rd)` (no clamp), `pow(grad,E)` pair, corona from `gradpos` as a
DIVISOR `/(1+1.25*spec1)`, spec colour `(0.4,0.4,0.4)`, donor composite order
(`*=(2.2+shade)` collapsed — see below), depth ramps `clamp(d/0.57)`/`clamp(d/1.07)`,
3-octave donor-form foam kernels reusing waveMap. No `×8/×4` boosts, no
`pow(grad,24)` sheen (both refuted).

Port-art tuning (`inferred`, each calibrated by measurement grid/probe, caveats
inline; W01 donor literals preserved, never overwritten):
- `P_SPAN=230.0` (normal-field span; tilt spectrum calibrated for sparse glints),
  `OCTAVE_SIGMA` `[0,0,0,1,3,5,8,13,22]` (per-octave prefilter ≈ footprint/2;
  effective texel footprint ≤1.2 tex/px, emulating donor `miLinearMipmapLinear`);
- `SLOPE_GAIN=30.0`, `GRAD_BLUR=0.5` (pre-pow anti-cusp smoothing; emulates
  mipmapped GPU sampling of the `|sin|`-cusp field — without it every cusp zero
  becomes a 1px grad spike and pow() turns spikes into med-1px salt-and-pepper,
  measured), `GLINT_BLUR=0.8` (post-composite coherence blur);
- `BODY_LP=1.8` (body modulation smoothed for the visual gate; detail lives in
  normals), `BODY_A=7.0`, `BODY_L0=44.0` (`derived` anchor), 0.90·blur9
  large-scale flattening (keeps the ACF background flat; 10% broad variation
  kept for faint banding), body `swell_amp=0.0` + `K2_GAIN=1.0` (donor verbatim;
  the k2-boost and body-swell variants were measured and rejected, §3A);
- `SPEC_K=515.0`, `PIECE_EXP_SOFT/GLOSSY=260/180` (piece-level retune; donor
  170/120 pinned and recorded — precedent water_deep.md §3 recipe exponent
  400/gain 6; with donor exponents the CPU cusp field renders 2–3% single-pixel
  speckle instead of sparse coherent blobs, measured; DEVIATION from "slope/z
  only" guidance, documented here with evidence);
- `SUN_DIST=6.0`, sun azimuths 0°/35°, `FOAM_WEAK_GAIN=0.15`, `TEX_SCALE_S/T`
  applied as above, synthetic depths `PIECE_DEPTH_DEEP_M=2.0` /
  `PIECE_DEPTH_SHORE_M_MAX=0.6` (zero foam beyond 1.07 m by construction).

shadow inputs (port decision, no reflection layer).
