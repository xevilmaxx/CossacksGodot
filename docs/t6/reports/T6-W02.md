## 3. SPOT-CHECK FIX (coordinator visual review — both artifacts resolved)

### FIX-1: hard black jagged band at the sand/water interface — FIXED

- **Defect (`observed` on `t6_water_shore.png`):** a hard black (`RGB≈0`) jagged band
  sat exactly on the sand→wash boundary. The donor has no black band.
- **Root cause:** the first shore revision painted land, wash, water and foam but
  **never assigned the sand band** (`rgb` starts as zeros; the `sand` boolean mask
  had no corresponding paint statement). The unpainted strip rendered as pure black.
  (Coordinator's "zero-alpha gap / band-geometry gap" diagnosis was essentially right;
  the gap was an unpainted band, not an alpha gap.)
- **Change:** the sand band is now painted as a continuous dry→wet-sand gradient
  (`sand_hex` → `wet_hex`, darkening 1.0→0.82 toward the waterline, 2px feather into
  dry land), so the sequence dry sand → wet sand → wash → shallow water is gapless.
- **Before/after evidence:** before `t6_water_shore.png` (10:54 revision, black band
  present); after `app/shots/pieces/t6_water_shore.png` sha256
  `40a91db8…80a73bc9`. Measured: `black_px` (all channels <20) went from a visible
  band to **0** (`observed`); perpendicular profile at x=256 (every 32px):
  `(164,152,119)×6 → (166,154,121) → (161,148,116) → (179,185,172) → (199,213,206)
  → (193,207,200)…` — continuous, no black, no step (`observed`); longest
  constant-x boundary run **7px** ≤ 8 (`derived`).

### FIX-2: concentric ring pattern in the water body — REMOVED

- **Defect (`observed`):** the shore water body showed procedural concentric
  rings (radial-sine term, centre lower-right) — radial synthesis, NOT donor waves.
- **Root cause:** the first revision carried `water_shallow.md`'s older depiction
  approximation (radial-sine arcs over the body). Coordinator ruling: that spec
  section is superseded for the water body — donor waves are scrolled samples of
  `diffusewaves0` in 9-octave fBm with directional scroll (W01 §2 verbatim).
- **Change:** the radial-sine term is DELETED from the water body. The shore water
  body is now the SAME texture-sampled `water_map()` 9-octave donor field as the
  deep piece (same P_SPAN scale), modulating the hard-gate `#C3D1CA` plateau.
  No radial generator remains anywhere in the tool (verified by grep for
  `sin(` outside the donor swell/`water_map`/`nautic` forms).
- **Before/after evidence:** before `t6_water_shore.png` (rings visible, crest
  spacing 6px by construction); after `app/shots/pieces/t6_water_shore.png`
  (cellular fBm texture, no rings — visual `observed`). Consequence, reported
  honestly: the old M6 (6px crest median) / M7 (~33-level contrast) depiction
  metrics no longer apply to the body by design — measured crest spacing on the
  fBm field is 164px (FAIL as a ripple-arc metric) and p90–p20 contrast 13.1
  (FAIL vs 33). The coherent texture scale is instead the ACF peak at ~11px
  (deep piece, PASS). No pass manufactured.

## 4. Implementation summary (equations, constants, labels)

Donor equations implemented verbatim (`observed`, W01-pinned): `noise(p)`,
3-term f base with `m`, crossing sine swell, 9-octave loop (`p=m*p*0.947`,
`amp*=0.59`, `s*=-1.329`, shifts `(0.32t,0.24t)`/`(0.38t,-0.26t)`), central
differences at `dif=(0,0.01)` with `z=0.15`, per-pixel ray `rd=normalize(light-r0)`,
`grad=dot(n,rd)` (no clamp), `pow(grad,E)` pair, corona from `gradpos` as a
DIVISOR `/(1+1.25*spec1)`, spec colour `(0.4,0.4,0.4)`, donor composite order
(`*=(2.2+shade)` collapsed — see below), depth ramps `clamp(d/0.57)`/`clamp(d/1.07)`,
3-octave donor-form foam kernels reusing waveMap. No `×8/×4` boosts, no
`pow(grad,24)` sheen (both refuted).

Port-art tuning (`inferred`, each calibrated by measurement grid, caveats inline):
`P_SPAN=230.0` (ripple scale; ACF peak at lag 11), `OCTAVE_SIGMA`
`[0,0,0,1,3,5,8,13,22]` (per-octave prefilter ≈ footprint/2; effective texel
footprint ≤1.2 tex/px, emulating donor `miLinearMipmapLinear`),
`SLOPE_GAIN=30.0`, `GRAD_BLUR=0.5` (pre-pow anti-cusp smoothing; emulates
mipmapped GPU sampling of the `|sin|`-cusp field — without it every cusp zero
becomes a 1px grad spike and pow() turns spikes into med-1px salt-and-pepper,
measured), `GLINT_BLUR=0.8` (post-composite coherence blur), `BODY_LP=1.5`
(body modulation smoothed; donor body is smooth, detail lives in normals),
`BODY_A=7.0`, `BODY_L0=44.0` (`derived` anchor), `SPEC_K=490.0`,
`PIECE_EXP_SOFT/GLOSSY=260/180` (piece-level retune; donor 170/120 pinned and
recorded — precedent water_deep.md §3 recipe exponent 400/gain 6; with donor
exponents the CPU cusp field renders 2–3% single-pixel speckle instead of
sparse coherent blobs, measured; DEVIATION from "slope/z only" guidance,
documented here with evidence), `SUN_DIST=6.0`, sun azimuths 0°/35°,
`FOAM_WEAK_GAIN=0.15`, `TEX_SCALE_S/T` applied as above, synthetic depths
`PIECE_DEPTH_DEEP_M=2.0` / `PIECE_DEPTH_SHORE_M_MAX=0.6` (zero foam beyond
1.07 m by construction). Deviation note: `BODY_L0`/affine collapses the donor
intensity/reflection/`×(2.2+shade)` chain because a headless piece has no
terrain-colour/reflection/shadow inputs (port decision, no reflection layer).
