## 8. Top 3 concrete deficits and fixes (ranked from the A/B)

1. **Hard 64 px tile checkerboard / no seam blending.**
   *Observed:* every tile boundary is a sharp colour discontinuity; the donor
   ground is continuous. This is the single most visually damaging difference
   and is corroborated by the 1.47× block-gradient ratio.
   *Fix:* replace independent per-tile random block+crop choice (`tile_choice()`
   in R06 `terrain.rs`) with the donor's 124-slot Summer TileBlock assignment
   plus its 6-pass `tilesmooth` chain (`TileSmoothPasses=6` in `globalparams.cfg`;
   port data in `structs/tileblocks.json`, `structs/smooth_summer.json`), and
   feather/blend tile borders in the rasterizer. R06 already flagged the missing
   seam blending as open question 3.

2. **Synthetic periodic diagonal shading instead of donor relief lighting.**
   *Observed:* broad sinusoidal light/dark bands across the whole patch, obvious
   in `ab_luminance.png`; block-luminance std is 1.67× the donor's.
   *Fix:* drive the height field from donor relief sources
   (`tile_highground/lowground`, `localparams.cfg` constants) rather than the
   analytic `sin/cos` formula, derive normals from that field, and calibrate the
   light/ambient constants against a donor terrain patch; add real
   shadow/occlusion if the 2D pipeline can approximate it.

3. **Wrong tile-family mix and colour balance (too dark, too little green).**
   *Observed:* roughly half the tiles are tan cracked earth / dark brown rock,
   while the donor crop is almost entirely grass with rock only in the corner;
   mean R/G are 21.3/25.1 lower, mean luminance 22.6 lower, mean saturation
   0.079 lower; the donor has 74.3% of pixels in sat 0.5–0.7 vs Rust 48.4%.
   *Fix:* weight block selection to the donor Summer preset's grass-dominant
   mix; restrict `sandsea`/`sandrock` to genuine shoreline cells; add a
   calibration pass matching mean luminance/saturation to a donor truth patch.

Not top-3 but noted: the Rust patch contains no trees/decor (the donor crop has
a few saplings) — arguably outside a terrain-only gate; and the whole frame is
2D orthographic vs the donor's 3D perspective. Both are flagged UNVERIFIED for
this scope, not claimed fixed.
