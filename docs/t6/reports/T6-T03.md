### How the four visual failure modes were removed

- **No plank fence:** flutes come from rock1 downsampled to 128/96 px then
  upscaled (4–8× wider crests), mixed at two scales (70/30), horizontally
  warped by seeded 1-D noise (`WARP_AMP=11`), gently bent per column, compressed
  in depth, gated to 0.18–1.0 presence, and cut by a periodic horizontal
  stratification field plus three seeded ledge bands. Measured crest-spacing CV
  in the donor is 0.765; the render's crests are likewise non-uniform.
- **No black contour:** the rim is a binary hue transition softened only by the
  1024→512 LANCZOS downsample; the collar is a *subtractive* (equal-RGB) darkening
  `26·exp(-d/13)` levels, so it reads as a soft shadow while R−G still crosses in
  ≤3 px. There is no outline stroke.
- **No straight centerlines:** the rim is a three-octave seeded smooth field
  (13/5.5/2 px at native) blurred by 2 px; no polyline is used.
- **No flat lighting:** a piecewise LIT→MID→SHADOW vertical ramp rooted at the
  rim (ratio 1.97) plus a broad x-axis lit-crest/dark-recess field (±20%) gives
  a bright central crest against shadowed flanks, like the donor.

## 3. Honest visual judgement (A/B, read both PNGs)

I read `t6_shore_rock.png`, `t6_shore_rock_compare.png` and
`t6_shore_rock_facecrop.png` as images, and the donor zoom probes
`t6_t03_donor_wall_zoom.png` / `t6_t03_donor_lit_zoom.png`.

**What now reads correctly:** an olive-tan rock wall with irregular wavy vertical
flutes of visibly unequal spacing and depth, clear horizontal ledges cutting
the flutes, a bright pale crest in the centre, dark recesses to the left and
right, a textured olive grass cap, a gently curved organic rim, and a soft
shadowed collar at the grass contact. The old `shore_rock.png` baseline (hard
rectangular outline, uniform "plank" fill, floating boulder) is gone. This is a
2D proxy that reads as rock, not diagram art.

**What is still weaker than the donor (honest caveats):** the donor crop shows a
single large *curved* wall face with conifers/yellow weeds at the edges and
grass tufts visibly overhanging a darker collar; the piece is a flat 2D texture
with no vegetation (the `rock_sea1` stand-in remains a small pasted-looking
accent) and its large-scale geometry is a broad lighting field rather than true
wall curvature. The flutes, while irregular, are more uniformly "wavy curtain"
than the donor's few bold, high-contrast crests. These are limitations of a
single 512² 2D proxy, not metric failures; a combo/integration capture must
verify them in-engine (consistent with `verdict_shore.md` §7).
