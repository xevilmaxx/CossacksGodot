  fades are broader and curved. The progression grainy tan sand → pale gray-tan wash → pale
  gray-cyan water matches the donor crop and is clearly not flat teal.

  caveats: a pale inner pool band and dark right-hand strip are still visible as 2D depth
  proxies, and the donor crop is a 6× upscale of an 80×34 JPEG region, so exact texture identity
  is not claimable. No in-engine claim is made from this tile.
