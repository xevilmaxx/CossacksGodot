### 7.2 Q2 read-only refinement — HOME candidates cannot fix the drift

Probes: `%TEMP%\opencode\t6u02\{home_candidates.py,home_candidates.json,
drift_by_action.py}`. For each key, seven HOME definitions were measured:
`idle_first` (current), `idle_median`, `idle_rest_first`, `walk_first`,
`attack0_first`, nearest-origin frame, and global-median root position.

**Derived (exact):** de-travel replaces each frame's foot centroid `f_fr` with
`f_fr − (root_fr − HOME) = (f_fr − root_fr) + HOME`. Pairwise drift subtracts two
such terms, so **HOME cancels identically**. Observed: all 7 candidates give
exactly the same global drift for pikeman (0.0761), cossack (1.0306), croat
(0.4874). HOME changes only the *absolute* placement (e.g. choosing HOME=(0,0)
puts the cossack idle foot 2.06 m off the origin); it cannot stabilize the feet.

Localization with the current HOME (`drift_by_action.py`, `observed`):

| Key | walk | idle | attack | death (per-clip ref; fall, pin N/A) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cossack | **1.0306 m** | 0.0800 m | 0.7673 m | 3.0563 m |
| croat | 0.0417 m | 0.0152 m | **0.4874 m** | 3.4273 m |

The intra-clip root span tracks the foot drift (cossack walk root span 1.0649 m;
croat attack 0.4919 m), i.e. the rider's bone-0 track roams within the clip
while the foot-driving bones do not follow it. This is consistent with mounted
riders (horse gait / stirrup motion), **inferred**, not proven by rendered
pixels. No fix attempted.
