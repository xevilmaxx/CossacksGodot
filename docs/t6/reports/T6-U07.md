### 3.3 Frigate ortho delta — VERIFIER DERIVATION ARTEFACT, RESOLVED (D)

**Parent ruling:** the delta was apples-to-oranges, not a sheet defect. The
comparison is between a rounded-input projection and a raw-input projection;
the fix belongs in the derivation method, not the gate. The 0.002 tolerance was
NOT changed and no value was hard-coded. In this worker's owned `naval.py`,
`donor_truth()` now projects with the **same raw center** as `bake_hull()` /
`bake_crew()` and rounds `center`/`ortho` only when writing JSON.

| Quantity | Value | Label |
|---|---|---|
