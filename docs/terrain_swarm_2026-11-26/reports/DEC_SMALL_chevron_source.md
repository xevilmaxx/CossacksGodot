## Verdict

Chevrons are **(b) face-mode promotion + (c) UV/projection sampling**, not (a) authored rock art and not primarily (d) height geometry.

- (a) Authored rock/test art — REJECTED as the chevron source. Donor cliff tiles are plain rock photographs: `data/terrain/tiles/tiles.mat:71-100` (`rock1`, `rock4`, `rock7`, `rock8` → `rockN.dds`); `tiles.mat:142-213` `rockNvf`/`rockNvs` reuse the **same** `rockN.dds` image and differ only by `refkey=verticalfront|verticalside` (`refurl=...\data\terrain\ref\reftile.mat`). The `test2`/`test3` art appears only in the road branch (`texturemap.inc:634-636`), not the cliff branch. Same image on all three face variants cannot itself draw nested diagonal chevrons; it only reveals the wrong projection when sampled with the wrong planes/scale.
- (d) Height/stamp geometry — NOT the pattern source. Flat-ground 2 m grid is fixed per task context and the relief grid carries 9,164 unique heights, so terracing/quantisation is out. Zero-grid mountain interiors with vertex-mask stamps remain a real port geometry deviation, but a height error produces smooth wrong slopes or mesa walls, not large nested **diagonal** chevron/stairstep bands aligned to the face-projection axes. Height stays a secondary suspect, not the chevron generator.
- (b)+(c) — SUPPORTED. The port promotes **every** `|dh|>cDiff` cell to face mode 1/2 by dominant axis, while the donor only stamps a rock tile on **collision-tagged** steep cells and leaves all other cells on the base tile; the port shader then samples those promoted cells with the eye-linear face projection (Scale 0.125, S=X/T=Z or S=Y/T=Z). A ground-UV pixel field re-sampled through a 1/8-scale world-XZ/YZ projection on cells the donor never promoted is exactly the large nested diagonal chevron/stairstep signature in `frame_0012.png`.
