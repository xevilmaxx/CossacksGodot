- **[O]** literal report/file line or read-only filesystem check; **[D]** deterministic consequence of cited lines/arithmetic; **[I]** interpretation with caveat; **[U]** unresolved; `PORT APPROXIMATION` = explicit port substitute. Every donor claim carries a path:line or an evidence id from §3.
- "**Runnable now**" means *the tool exists and its inputs exist today*. A row may be "tool present, input absent" or "owner-only" — neither is a PASS. V21 ran **no** Godot process and **no** runner subcommand, and executed no donor binary.

- Severity is **red** only where the pass criterion is a donor-grounded structural/determinism invariant or a hard port-integrity rule. Every numeric *appearance/range* band is **advisory**, even when the number looks precise.
